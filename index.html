Full Stack SaaS Next.js

DevPulse

DevPulse is a full-stack uptime monitoring platform I built for developers and small teams. It lets users track websites and APIs, view response-time history, detect outages, and share public status pages. The app includes a protected dashboard, incident tracking, manual and scheduled health checks, and email alert support for downtime and recovery.

I built it with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma, and PostgreSQL, with Auth.js for authentication, QStash for scheduled checks, Resend for alerts, and Recharts for monitoring visuals. The project was designed to demonstrate production-style engineering: secure server-side monitoring, database design, background job workflows, and resilient failure handling.
